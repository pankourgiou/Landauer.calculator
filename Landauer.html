What this does and doesn't say

The limit is about erasing, meaning steps that can't be run backwards. Computing that never throws information away (reversible computing) can, in principle, get under it. And you only reach the floor by erasing infinitely slowly; anything faster costs more.

It was first measured in a lab in 2012 (Bérut and colleagues, in Nature), using a single microscopic bead held in a laser trap. A decimal digit carries log210 ≈ 3.32 bits, which is what the "decimal digits" unit uses.
